How to Find Your Starting Pitch
  1. Look at the first note of your vocal line on the score (skip any piano intro).
  2. Is the note head on a line (a line passes through its center) or in a space (floating between two lines with no line through it)?
  3. Count from the bottom of the treble clef staff using this guide:
  1. Find that note on the piano below and click it to hear it.
  2. Once you’ve found your note, click Set as Starting Pitch to save it.
